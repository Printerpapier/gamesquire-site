How to choose a board game in a minute

  1. Get your games into GameSquireAdd games by search, scan a barcode for free, or photograph a box (5 scans to try for free). If your games already live on BoardGameGeek, import your collection in one step.
  2. Open Mood & filters and tap a moodSix moods are ready on your shelf, a friend's shelf, a group's shelf and your household's shared shelf. One tap hides everything that does not fit.
  3. Narrow it downAdd the number of players, how long you have, or how heavy you want it to feel, and tags. The list only shows games that fit all of it.
  4. Compare shelves when you play with othersOpen a friend's shelf and tap Owned by both to see only the games you each have. In a saved group of friends, Owned by everyone does the same for the whole table.

The six moods

  • Quick Fix: short and easy.
  • Deep Dive: heavy and long.
  • Team Up: win as a team (co-op games).
  • Party Starter: for big groups.
  • Date Night: just for two.
  • Family Table: good for families.

Why start from the evening, not the list

A game list tells you what you own. It does not tell you what you can play right now with these people. GameSquire starts from the evening: how many of you there are, how much time you have and what kind of game everyone is in the mood for. Moods use each game's player count, play time, weight and category from BoardGameGeek, so they work without you tagging anything. A game with missing weight data is left out of the moods that depend on it rather than guessed at.

Plan around the whole household

Up to five people can share one household shelf, with everyone's own ratings and play logs staying personal. Apply a mood to the shared shelf and you are choosing from every game in the house.

Questions

How does GameSquire know which games are quick or heavy?

It uses each game's data from BoardGameGeek: player counts, play time, weight and category. A game without that data is left out of the moods that need it instead of being guessed at.

Can I see which games a friend and I both own?

Yes. Add them as a friend, open their shelf and tap Owned by both. A friend's shelf also tells you how many games they have and how many of those you both own.

Can I find a board game my whole group owns?

Yes. Save a group of friends and open the group view, then use Owned by everyone to see the games every person has. Mood and the other filters work there too.

Do I need to pay to use the mood filter?

No. Moods, filters and shelf matching with friends are part of the free app, which has no ads. Knight membership is optional.

What board game should we play with just two people?

Tap the Date Night mood to see the games on your shelf made for two. You can then narrow it by how long you have and how heavy you want it to feel.
