What it does

Everything a shelf app should be, plus the part they all skip

Track what you own, remember what you've played, and the part every other tracker leaves out. See it all against your friends' shelves too.

Your shelf, your way

Log every game you own and everything you're hoping to get next. Filter by player count, playtime, difficulty, or type, and stack them together, so four players and under an hour narrows your shelf instantly.

Every play, remembered

Track who played, who won, how long it ran, and whatever's worth remembering about the game. Tag the friends who were at the table and the play lands in their history too. No double entry.

Match your shelf with a friend's

Add friends, see their collection, and instantly spot the overlap. No more group-chat archaeology to figure out what everyone actually owns before game night.

Track the expansions too

See every expansion that exists for a game on your shelf, not just the ones you already own, so you always know what to add next.

Most shelf-tracking apps stop at your own collection, a private list only you ever look at. GameSquire starts there too, but doesn't stop there: the moment you add a friend, your shelf becomes useful to someone else. A board game collection is more fun when it's not just yours.
