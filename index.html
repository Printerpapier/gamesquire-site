What it does

What should we play tonight? GameSquire answers that.

Not just a catalogue of what you own. Add your friends and your household and the question changes from "what do I have" to "what can we actually play right now." Already tracking on BoardGameGeek? Bring your collection, ratings and plays over in one step.

Pick tonight's game by mood

Quick Fix, Deep Dive, Date Night, Family Table. One tap filters your shelf, a friend's or your household's down to what actually fits tonight.

Match your shelf with a friend's

Add friends, see their collection and instantly spot the overlap, plus their wishlist before you buy a gift. No more group-chat archaeology before game night.

Share one shelf with your household

Up to 5 people, one shared collection. Everyone's own ratings and play logs stay personal, the shelf itself stays shared, just like the games on it.

Every play, remembered

Scores, co-op and solo outcomes, wishlist and every expansion for what's on your shelf. Tag the friends who were at the table and it lands in their history too.

Plenty of apps are good at recording what you own and what you played. GameSquire is for the moment before that, when everyone is at the table and nobody can decide. Add your friends and your household, pick a mood, and choose from what's left.

GameSquire is in public beta right now, free on iOS, Android and web. The full 1.0 release, with Premium, doesn't have a set date yet. Leave your email at the top of this page if you'd rather wait, and we'll tell you the moment it ships.

What does it cost?

The core app is free to use, forever, with no ads, ever: your shelf, play log, friends, collection matching and up to 5 people sharing one household shelf. An optional Premium subscription adds a guided rulebook assistant, box scanning and more for people who want the extra toolkit around game night, pricing will be announced closer to launch.

What platforms is GameSquire on?

iOS, Android and web, all live now, all from one account.

I already track my games on BoardGameGeek or in another app. Do I have to start over?

No. GameSquire imports your BoardGameGeek collection, wishlist, ratings and logged plays in one step, including plays another app has posted to BoardGameGeek for you. Only what's on BoardGameGeek comes across, so extras another app keeps to itself, like custom stats, stay there. Running the import again later is safe and won't create duplicates. GameSquire is built to sit alongside the tools you already use, so keep logging wherever you like and open GameSquire when it's time to pick a game with the people around you.

Is my data private?

If you leave your email for launch news, it is only ever used to send you that. We won't sell it or hand it to anyone else. No ads means no ad trackers watching what you do in the app. And your collection is never stuck here: it's built on BoardGameGeek's own data, and your shelf and wishlist export to CSV for free, whenever you want.
