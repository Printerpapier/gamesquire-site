What it does

What should we play tonight? GameSquire answers that.

Not just a catalogue of what you own. Add your friends and your household and the question changes from "what do I have" to "what can we actually play right now."

Pick tonight's game by mood

Quick Fix, Deep Dive, Date Night, Family Table. One tap filters your shelf, a friend's or your household's down to what actually fits tonight.

Match your shelf with a friend's

Add friends, see their collection and instantly spot the overlap, plus their wishlist before you buy a gift. No more group-chat archaeology before game night.

Share one shelf with your household

Up to 5 people, one shared collection. Everyone's own ratings and play logs stay personal, the shelf itself stays shared, just like the games on it.

Every play, remembered

Scores, co-op and solo outcomes, wishlist and every expansion for what's on your shelf. Tag the friends who were at the table and it lands in their history too.

"What should we play tonight" is a decade-old, still-unanswered question on every board game forum. GameSquire ends the back-and-forth: add your friends and your household, filter by mood and the debate is over before it starts.

When does it launch?

GameSquire is in active development right now. There's no launch date yet. The waitlist is the fastest way to hear as soon as there is one.

What does it cost?

The core app is free to use, forever, with no ads, ever: your shelf, play log, friends, collection matching and up to 5 people sharing one household shelf. An optional Premium subscription adds a guided rulebook assistant, box scanning and more for people who want the extra toolkit around game night, pricing will be announced closer to launch.

What platforms will it be on?

iOS, Android and the web, all from one account.

Is my data private?

Your waitlist email is only ever used to send you launch updates. We won't sell it or hand it to anyone else. No ads means no ad trackers watching what you do in the app. And your collection is never stuck here: it's built on BoardGameGeek's own data and everything exports to CSV for free, whenever you want.
