Log your board game plays

Log a game in seconds while the pieces are still on the table: who played, who won, how long it took. Your plays turn into stats you can look back on, and the friends you tag get the play in their history too.

How logging a play works

  1. Pick the gameOpen Log a Play and choose a game from your shelf or search for it. You can also start from the game's own page.
  2. Add the players and scoresEnter a score for each player and the highest score gets the winner's star. Ties are easy, and co-op and solo games are covered too. Prefer not to type? Take a photo of the score sheet (5 to try for free), check the names and numbers, and the photo is not kept.
  3. Add what you want to rememberSet the date and length, note any expansions you played with, and say where you played. The place and any notes are private to you.
  4. Tag the friends who were thereA tagged friend can accept or reject the play. Once they accept, it shows in their stats as if they had logged it themselves, and later changes show in both logs.
  5. Save it, or log another roundAfter saving, log the next round with the same game, date and players already filled in.

A timer that keeps running

Tap Start a play on a game's page and a timer runs while you play, even if you close the app. Pause it for a snack break so that time is left out. On your phone it also shows on the lock screen with the game's box art (on Android as a notification), where you can pause, resume or stop it. Stopping it opens the game and asks before it logs the play.

Board game stats and win rate

Stats shows your win rate, how much of your shelf you have actually played, your top five games, your co-op and solo record, your weekly streak and plays per month. Open a friend's profile to see your head-to-head record, and a game's page shows your record on that game. A People you play with list shows everyone from your plays and who won.

Questions

Is logging plays free?

Yes. Logging plays, scores and your own stats are free, with no ads. Knight membership is optional and adds deeper Insights, score sheets and the fuller Year's Tally.

Can I log co-op and solo games?

Yes. You can log whether a co-op game was won or lost, and a solo play counts in your record. Stats keeps your co-op and solo record separately.

Can I add the expansions I played with?

Yes. Add the expansions you used when you log the play. An expansion's own page shows how many plays of its base game you used it in.

What happens when I tag a friend in a play?

They are asked to accept or reject it. If they accept, the play counts in their stats too. If you log several rounds with the same friend, they see one invite they can accept with a single tap.

Can I import my board game plays from BoardGameGeek?

Yes. Type your BoardGameGeek username and your logged plays come across with their dates, length, players, scores and places. Plays you already logged in GameSquire by hand are kept.
