GameSquire

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Who we are

GameSquire is made by Squire Builds, a sole proprietorship operated by Vincent Demeulenaere, based in Belgium. We're the data controller for the information described below. Contact us at support@squirebuilds.com with any question or request.

2. What we collect

If you sign up on this site for news of GameSquire 1.0: your email address. If you use the GameSquire app: your email address and password (handled by our authentication provider, we never see your password in plain text), profile info (display name, username, and avatar you choose), and your data (the games you add to your shelf or wishlist, the plays you log (date, duration, notes, who played), and your friend connections). We don't collect location, contacts, or device identifiers beyond what's needed to keep you signed in.

3. Why we collect it

To run the app: showing your collection back to you, letting friends see what you own and what plays you've logged with them, and keeping your account secure. If you signed up on this site, also to tell you when GameSquire 1.0 launches. We also use your email address for account emails you cannot opt out of, such as confirming your address or resetting your password, and occasionally to tell you about GameSquire itself: a new release, a significant new feature, or the move out of beta and what Premium costs. You can unsubscribe from those updates at any time with the link in each one, and doing so never affects your account. We also use privacy-focused product analytics (cookieless on this website, tied to your account inside the app) to see which pages and features people actually use (for example, adding a game, logging a play, or sending a friend request), so we can improve GameSquire. Inside the app, these events are tied to your account, not to any advertising profile. Inside the mobile app, this can include session recordings of your on-screen activity, used only to find and fix bugs; password fields are automatically hidden in these recordings. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. GameSquire has no ads and no ad tracking of any kind.

4. Who can see what

Inside the app, your profile (display name, username, avatar) is visible to other signed-in users so they can find and add you as a friend. Your shelf, wishlist, and play log are visible only to accepted friends, in the ways the app is designed to show them (e.g. "games you both own"). Notes you write on a play are visible to anyone who can see that play. The email address you leave for 1.0 launch news is never shown to anyone else.

5. Where it's stored

We use Supabase, a third-party infrastructure provider, to host the app's database and handle authentication, and PostHog (hosted in the EU) to record the product-analytics events and session recordings described in section 3. When the rules assistant is available (see section 6), the questions you ask and the rulebook it reads are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate an answer. If we add paid subscriptions, billing is handled by Apple or Google directly; we don't see or store your payment details. Purchases in the mobile apps would also be processed through RevenueCat, which keeps a record of each purchase linked to your account, and tips paid on the web would be processed by Stripe, which handles your payment details on its own checkout page. If you sign up on this site for 1.0 launch news, the form sends only your email address to Formspree, which receives the signup on our behalf.

6. Rulebooks and the rules assistant

GameSquire keeps a library of rulebooks so its rules assistant can answer questions about a game. Most of them come from publishers' own websites. You can also upload a rulebook PDF yourself. Before you do you confirm that you have the right to share the file and that it is read by an AI to answer questions for other players of the game. We check that the file is a rulebook for that game and store it. We then use it to answer the questions of every player of that game. The file is sent to Anthropic's Claude API, our AI provider, only to check it and to answer questions.

Other players are never shown your name or your file. The app only tells them that a rulebook was uploaded by a player and has not been checked against the publisher. You can download the rulebooks you uploaded from the rulebook screen in the app. We record when you confirmed that you have the right to share a file, and that record is not shown to anyone else. The files of refused or replaced uploads are deleted 30 days after that decision. If you delete your account we delete your uploads that were not approved. Approved uploads stay in the shared library without your name attached, because other players rely on them. If a publisher asks us not to use their rulebook we switch the rules assistant off for that game.

The questions you ask the assistant and its answers are stored with your account so you can see your recent questions. They count towards your daily and monthly limit. If you use the report button on a rulebook we keep your report (the rulebook, the problem you chose, your message and your account) so we can review it, and we email it to ourselves. A report never removes anything by itself.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account is active, or until you unsubscribe from 1.0 launch news. You can delete your app account at any time from Profile → Delete account, which permanently removes your profile, collection, play logs, and friend connections.

8. Your rights

Under EU/Belgian data protection law (GDPR), you can request access to, correction of, or a copy of your data, and can request deletion at any time (see above, or email us). If you believe we've mishandled your data, you can lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be).

9. Children

GameSquire isn't directed at children under 16. If you believe a child has created an account or signed up for 1.0 launch news without appropriate consent, contact us and we'll remove it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
