GameSquire

Someone wants to compare board game shelves with you.

GameSquire is a free board game tracker. Once you both have it you can see what the other owns, find the games you both have and log the ones you play together.

Get GameSquire, free:

  • iPhone or iPad, via TestFlight
  • Android, on Google Play
  • Or just use it in your browser

Already have GameSquire? Open it, go to Social, and add them there.

No ads, ever. The core is free and your data is yours: see the privacy policy.
